# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- being kind, patient and respectful, especially with people who are learning;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, about the code and not the person;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected and learning from
  them;
- focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission;
- any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews) and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@MITRAKER](https://github.com/MITRAKER),
through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Every report will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the person reporting will
be respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that don't follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Depending on the
impact, the response ranges from a private warning, explaining why the behavior was
inappropriate, to a permanent ban from the project.
